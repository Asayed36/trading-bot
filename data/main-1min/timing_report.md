## How to read this

These tables describe past data, nothing more. A good-looking hour or weekday is a lead to test on later days, not a rule: with only a few days of data a pattern is usually luck (look at every n, and at the buckets marked "too few to judge"). Nothing in this report changes the bot: no buy, sell, filter or setting reads it.

Tokens are placed by the UTC time their pool was created. 223 of 7752 rows have no creation time and are left out of the hour, weekday and block tables. "Alive" needs liquidity of at least $30,000 (config.toml [filters] min_liquidity_usd). A token DexScreener no longer has a pair for has no price and is left out of the medians, so the medians describe tokens still listed (and flatter the real picture). † = fewer than 30 behind that number.
